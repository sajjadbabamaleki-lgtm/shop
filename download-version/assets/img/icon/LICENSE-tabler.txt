The basket icon on the shop's product cards is `shopping-bag-plus` from
Tabler Icons. Unlike the category icons beside it, it is not a file: it is
inline SVG in storefront/resources/views/shop/card.blade.php, because it sits
inside a button and takes its colour from the text around it — an <img> cannot
inherit currentColor. This notice is here because that is where the other two
are, and because the obligation travels with the copy either way.

The drawing is unchanged from the set.
